## Original concept

**LUCENT**: a fictional portable cinema projector with dark aluminum, readable edge chamfers, cooling ribs/ventilation, one deep glass lens, and controlled warm light. It is an independent design study, with no purchase flow, real certification or invented performance promise.

Core line: **Small object. Endless cinema.**

Story: discover the silhouette; move into optical layers; read the physical object; enter the light; choose a material and cinematic mood; leave with design notes. The main scroll climax approaches the lens and opens into a full-screen cool blue cinematic world. All product/environment rendering should be original code-native work.

## Motion contract

- Continuous camera rotation/position/scale is driven by normalized scroll progress. Keep normal document scrolling.
- Maintain legible composition at chapter boundaries and during interrupted/reversed scroll.
- Warm-to-neutral-to-blue lighting supports the story; no unrelated perpetual colour cycling.
- No compulsory dragging, hover-only information, cursor replacement or autoplay sound.
- Finish/scene switches handle repeated and interrupted activation, with clear selected state.
- Reduced motion: static camera, no parallax or forced smooth scrolling, all chapter content readable.
- WebGL fallback: retain product depiction, narrative, labels and controls through static/SVG rendering.
