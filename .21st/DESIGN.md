# LUCENT design context

LUCENT is an original fictional portable cinematic projector. The parent's selected direction is grounded in actual reference frames reviewed in Linux by the parent and separate analyst. This desktop art-director role has not personally viewed the video.

## Visual identity

One dark aluminum object, one deep glass lens, continuous cinematic camera choreography. Near-black negative space, soft white editorial type, warm amber rim/beam resolving into cool blue cinema. Oversized LUCENT lettering is a background layer, not a substitute for product detail.

Headline: **Small object. Endless cinema.**

## Composition

The hero puts an immense three-quarter projector on the right and short white copy in the left lower quadrant. Thin top navigation and a small warm CTA provide orientation. Mobile uses separate text/object bands to retain legibility. The full story moves from silhouette to lens macro, physical construction, blue projected world, interactive finishes/scenes, then compact design notes.

## Interaction

`Explore the lens` scrolls to a real chapter. Finish and scene buttons visibly update the render and expose selected state. Download opens a real bundled concept sheet. There is no checkout or fictional backend.

## Tokens

- Background: `#07090b`
- Foreground: `#f2f3ee`
- Secondary text: `#a8adb2`
- Fine rules: `#30363b`
- Warm accent: `#ff8347`
- Cool accent: `#93c8ff`
- Metal: `#333b42` to `#b8bec3`
- Typography: system sans, heavy display, tight tracking; system monospace for technical captions.
- Desktop gutter: `clamp(24px, 5vw, 88px)`; mobile gutter `20px`.
- Motion easing: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`; controls about 180 ms, large state switches about 600 ms.

## Required safeguards

Reduced motion: static camera, no parallax, all narrative readable. WebGL fallback retains product depiction and controls. Keyboard-visible focus, semantic controls, 44 px touch targets, adequate contrast, no horizontal overflow. No unlicensed source branding/assets, paid generation, fake certifications, performance promises or sales.

See `reports/art-direction.md` for provenance and `design.json` for machine-readable decisions.
