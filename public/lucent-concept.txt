LUCENT P / 01
A world beyond the frame.

INDEPENDENT DESIGN CONCEPT — 2026

An original study of a portable cinema projector, explored through industrial
form, light, material, and a continuous interactive camera journey.

FORM
A softly chamfered aluminum shell, a prominent concentric glass lens,
repeating ventilation ribs, a tactile top dial, and a soft-touch base.

FINISHES
Obsidian: quiet graphite aluminum.
Lunar: a bright, cool aluminum interpretation.
Ember: a warm copper interpretation.

WORLDS
Blue hour, Afterglow, and Far beyond are original procedural environments.

ABOUT THIS STUDY
This is a fictional product design concept, not a manufactured product or
an offer for sale. No certifications, measured optical performance, or
commercial availability are claimed.

The projector geometry, fallback illustration and cinematic environments
are original code-native assets. The supplied reference videos informed
composition and motion only; their product imagery is not redistributed.
