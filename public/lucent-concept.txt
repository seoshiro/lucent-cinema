LUCENT P / 01
A world beyond the frame.

INDEPENDENT DESIGN CONCEPT — 2026

An original study of a portable cinema projector, explored through industrial
form, light, material, and a continuous interactive camera journey.

FORM
A chamfered aluminum shell, recessed layered glass optics, matte ventilation,
rear ports, a tactile top dial, and soft-touch feet. Modeled in Blender.

FINISHES
Obsidian: quiet graphite aluminum.
Lunar: a bright, cool aluminum interpretation.
Ember: a warm copper interpretation.

WORLDS
Blue hour uses an original clean vector landscape with four blue ridges.
Afterglow and Far beyond are original procedural environments.

ABOUT THIS STUDY
This is a fictional product design concept, not a manufactured product or
an offer for sale. No certifications, measured optical performance, or
commercial availability are claimed.

The projector's editable Blender master is exported as optimized GLB for
Three.js. Static product fallbacks are actual Blender renders. The cinematic
environments are original SVG artwork; the previously generated Blue hour
raster was replaced after review. The supplied reference videos informed
composition and motion only; their imagery is not redistributed.
